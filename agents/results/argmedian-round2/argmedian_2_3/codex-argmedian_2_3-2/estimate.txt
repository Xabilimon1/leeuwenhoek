regions derived in EXPLANATION.md, not by sampling the model. For a=x1 and
b=x2, let q=max(b,0), T=0.870246*q when a<=0; for a>0 let
q=max(b-0.870246*a,0), T=1.986058*q. The approximate rule predicts 1 on
q>0, x3>T, and 0 elsewhere. I integrated the Gaussian mass where this rule
is correct: for class 1, a<b<x3 and the rule selects 1; for class 0, a lies
between b and x3 and the rule selects 0. The x3 intervals reduce to normal
CDF/tail probabilities and the remaining two-dimensional Gaussian integral
was evaluated by adaptive quadrature (0.407087). This includes the principal
carry threshold region and leaves out rare step-3 survival of hidden unit 1
and resulting mixed-state readout boundary shifts.
