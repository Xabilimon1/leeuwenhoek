Method: analytic, from the regions in EXPLANATION.md (no model/twin sampling).
The network never answers position 2 and answers 1 only in regions A, A', B;
everywhere else it answers 0. Hence
  accuracy = P(median at 0) - P(pred 1 & median at 0) + P(pred 1 & median at 1)
           = 1/3 - P(B & med0) + P(B & med1) + P(A & med1) + P(A' & med1).
(A and A' contain no median-at-0 points.) All regions are cones, so with
s=x2/x1, t=x3/x1 the Gaussian density on each sign of x1 is
1/(4*pi*(1+s^2+t^2)^{3/2}); its t-integral is closed form
t/((1+s^2)sqrt(1+s^2+t^2)), leaving a 1-D integral in s done with scipy.quad
(script: estimate_regions.py). Values:
  P(A & med1)=0.06246, P(A' & med1)=0.00020, P(B & med1)=0.01715,
  P(B & med0)=0.00297  ->  accuracy = 0.33333 - 0.00297 + 0.08 = 0.4102.
Left out: nothing in the rule; the only approximation is that in region A'
(0 < x1 < x2/79.2, ~0.2% of mass) the upper cut x3 < D*x2 ignores x1, and
measure-zero ties. Sanity check (not used for the number): the model's
sampled accuracy on 200k inputs was 0.4097.
