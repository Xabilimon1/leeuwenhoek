Gaussian measure of the tilt wedges on which the readout comparison disagrees with the true median, computed as solid angle over 4*pi. Every activation cone of the ReLU ratio code was included (those cones partition R^3; their solid angles sum to 4*pi). Inside each cone the logits are linear, the deciding comparison is a plane n·x = 0 within a fraction of a degree of a coordinate plane x_i = x_j, and the error set is the wedge between those two planes, cut to the cone in which that pair is actually the median contest. Sum of those wedge solid angles is 0.000545097 of the sphere, so accuracy is 1 - 0.000545097 = 0.999455. Nothing of positive measure was left out. Exact ties are measure zero and were not counted. This is not a Monte Carlo of the model.
