The estimate is derived by computing the Gaussian measure of the error regions identified in our mechanistic explanation.

Because the neural network is homogeneous of degree 0 (it has zero biases and consists of linear projections and positively homogeneous ReLUs), its predictions depend solely on the direction x / ||x|| on the unit 2-sphere S^2. Under standard i.i.d. Gaussian inputs, the directional distribution is uniform on S^2 with total surface area 4*pi.

The network's errors correspond to narrow boundary bands around the great circles where pairs of inputs coincide:
1. Primary error region (x2 ~ x3): The boundary between median positions 1 and 2 is the great circle plane x2 = x3 (unit normal n_23 = (0, 1, -1)/sqrt(2)). Along this circle, whenever x1 is not between x2 and x3, the network's recurrent state creates nearly balanced activations between outer and inner units (u ~ v). This residual ratio falls into the readout dead zone [0.757, 1.188] where logit 0 wins, outputting position 0 instead of 1 or 2. The angular half-width of this band is alpha_23 = GAP_23 / sqrt(2) ~ 0.0175 / sqrt(2) ~ 0.01237 rad. The area of a spherical strip of half-width alpha on S^2 is 4*pi*sin(alpha), so the fractional area is sin(alpha_23) ~ 0.012374.
2. Secondary error region (x1 ~ x3): Near the great circle x1 = x3, when position 0 is the true median, activation leakage causes logit 2 to override logit 0 within an angular half-width alpha_13 = GAP_13 / sqrt(2) ~ 0.0050 / sqrt(2) ~ 0.00354 rad. Since position 0 is the true median on 1/3 of the sphere (2 of the 6 spherical triangles), this contributes (1/3) * sin(alpha_13) ~ 0.001179.
3. Tertiary error region (x1 ~ x2): Near the great circle x1 = x2, when position 0 is the true median, activation leakage causes logit 1 to override logit 0 within an angular half-width alpha_12 = GAP_12 / sqrt(2) ~ 0.0028 / sqrt(2) ~ 0.00198 rad, contributing (1/3) * sin(alpha_12) ~ 0.000660.

Total error probability is the sum of these disjoint boundary measures:
P(error) = 0.012374 + 0.001179 + 0.000660 = 0.014213.
Estimated accuracy = 1 - P(error) = 0.985787 ~ 0.9858.

Error regions included:

Error regions left out:
- Higher-order corner intersections near the poles (+- (1, 1, 1)/sqrt(3)) where multiple boundaries intersect simultaneously (which have spherical measure < 1e-4).
- Very rare misclassifications between positions 1 and 2 (e.g. 1 -> 2 or 2 -> 1, which account for < 2% of errors).
