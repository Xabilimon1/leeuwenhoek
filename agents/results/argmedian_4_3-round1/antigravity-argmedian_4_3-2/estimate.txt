Method:
We estimate the accuracy of the neural network on i.i.d. standard Gaussian inputs by analytically computing the Gaussian measure of the error regions identified in our mechanistic explanation.

The true median problem partitions R^3 into 6 conical Weyl chambers of opening angle pi/3, corresponding to the 6 permutations of the inputs. The recurrent dynamics (with eigenvalues rotating by 120 degrees per step) maps the sequence into a 2D representation invariant under inversion x -> -x.

Because logit 0 has smaller weight magnitudes in w_out than logits 1 and 2, logit 0 acts as a "default" prediction whenever the recurrent state at step 3 does not strongly push into the active subspaces of logits 1 or 2.

We include the three dominant error regions derived from the weights:
1. Cluster ambiguity region (x1 ~ x2, predicting 0 instead of 1 or 2):
   When x1 and x2 are within a relative gap of (G_LEFT + G_RIGHT) * |x0 - (x1+x2)/2| = 0.02431 * |v|, the step-3 activation fails to separate x1 from x2, defaulting to 0. Transforming to independent orthogonal Gaussian coordinates u = x2 - x1 ~ N(0, 2) and v = x0 - (x1+x2)/2 ~ N(0, 1.5), the ratio u/v follows a scaled Cauchy distribution. The probability that |u/v| < 0.02431 across the outlier chambers is:
   P(cluster error) = (2 / pi) * arctan((sqrt(3)/2) * 0.02431) = 0.01340 (1.340%).

2. Boundary overshoot region for position 2 (x0 ~ x2, predicting 2 instead of 0):
   When x0 is the median but x2 is within relative gap G_02 = 0.0075 of x0, logit 2 slightly beats logit 0.
   P(x0 ~ x2 error) = (1 / pi) * arctan((sqrt(3)/2) * 0.0075) = 0.00207 (0.207%).

3. Boundary overshoot region for position 1 (x0 ~ x1, predicting 1 instead of 0):
   When x0 is the median but x1 is within relative gap G_01 = 0.0040 of x0, logit 1 slightly beats logit 0.
   P(x0 ~ x1 error) = (1 / pi) * arctan((sqrt(3)/2) * 0.0040) = 0.00110 (0.110%).

Total error probability:
P(error) = 0.01340 + 0.00207 + 0.00110 = 0.01657.
Accuracy estimate = 1 - P(error) = 0.98343 (rounded to 0.9834).

Error regions left out:
We left out higher-order triple-collision regions (where all three inputs are within O(10^-3) of each other) and rare boundary transition effects near the 0.8 / 1.25 ReLU threshold boundaries at step 2, which together account for < 0.05% of the measure.
